**1. COMPANY PROFILE**

**Legal Transformation Partners (Pty) Ltd (TLP)** is a South African
emerging consultancy providing B-BBEE advisory and transformation
support to participants in the legal sector.

TLP\'s services are focused on helping legal entities understand and
respond to applicable B-BBEE and transformation requirements, including
the **Legal Sector Code of Good Practice**.

TLP provides advisory, assessment, preparation and
implementation-support services. TLP is **not a B-BBEE verification
agency** and does not issue B-BBEE verification certificates.

Our objective is to provide clients with practical information,
structured assessments and transformation planning that can assist them
in preparing for applicable B-BBEE measurement and verification
processes.

TLP\'s advisory work may reference the following framework, as

The **Legal Sector Code of Good Practice** was gazetted in terms of

conducting its advisory work.

**12. OUR CLIENT ONBOARDING PROCESS**

**STEP 1 --- INITIAL CONSULTATION**

TLP obtains an understanding of:

-   the legal entity;

-   professional structure;

-   services;

-   revenue category;

-   ownership;

-   workforce;

-   current B-BBEE position;

-   previous B-BBEE documentation;

-   transformation objectives; and

-   immediate business requirements.

**STEP 2 --- CONFIDENTIALITY**

Where appropriate, TLP may enter into a confidentiality or
non-disclosure agreement with the client before receiving confidential
business information.

**STEP 3 --- INFORMATION REQUEST**

TLP provides the client with a tailored information and document
checklist.

**STEP 4 --- THRESHOLD ASSESSMENT**

TLP assesses which category and requirements are applicable based on the
information provided.

**STEP 5 --- DOCUMENT REVIEW**

Relevant information and supporting documents are reviewed.

**STEP 6 --- B-BBEE HEALTH CHECK**

TLP assesses the client\'s current position against the applicable
framework.

**STEP 7 --- GAP ANALYSIS**

Potential gaps, missing evidence and transformation opportunities are
identified.

**STEP 8 --- TRANSFORMATION ROADMAP**

TLP prepares a practical roadmap setting out:

**Current Position → Gap → Required Action → Responsibility → Evidence →
Timeframe**

**STEP 9 --- IMPLEMENTATION SUPPORT**

Where included in the engagement, TLP assists the client with
implementing agreed actions.

**STEP 10 --- MONITORING**

Progress and supporting evidence can be reviewed periodically.

**STEP 11 --- VERIFICATION READINESS**

Where an independent verification process is applicable, TLP can assist
the client in organizing and preparing its information and supporting
documentation.

The final verification outcome remains the responsibility of the
independent verification process.

**15. THE LEGAL SECTOR TRANSFORMATION CONTEXT**

The Legal Sector Code establishes sector-specific transformation
requirements and targets relating to areas including ownership,
management, skills development, procurement and enterprise and supplier
development.

TLP\'s role is to help clients understand the requirements applicable to
them and develop practical responses.

The Code\'s requirements must always be considered in the context of:

-   the applicable entity category;

-   the client\'s legal and professional structure;

-   the relevant measurement period;

-   available supporting evidence;

-   the applicable provisions of the Code; and

-   any relevant official guidance.
